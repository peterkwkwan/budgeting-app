Privacy Policy for Fudget

App: Fudget (com.koolkrib.fudget)
Developer: Kool Krib
Effective date: October 23, 2026
Contact: kribkool@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Kool Krib (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Fudget mobile application (the “App”). Fudget is a personal budgeting app. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Fudget, we may collect:

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive identifiers provided by Google through our authentication provider (typically email address and basic profile information needed to create and maintain your account).
  • Budget and financial entries you create. Amounts, income/expense type, categories, optional notes, dates/times, and recurring-rule settings you enter in the App.
  • Profile settings. Such as your timezone, used to display dates and generate recurring entries correctly.
  • Partner-link data. If you use optional partner/family linking, we store the link relationship (for example invite codes and which accounts are linked) so we can show aggregate spending/income totals between linked accounts.
  • Voice input (on device). If you use voice entry, speech is converted to text using on-device Android speech recognition. We do not send your microphone audio to a separate cloud speech vendor for the current App version. The resulting text is treated like other transaction draft content you confirm and save.
  • Technical / session data. Information needed to keep you signed in and sync data securely (for example session tokens and standard app/network diagnostics generated by the platform or our backend).

We do not currently use third-party advertising SDKs or third-party product analytics SDKs in the App. If that changes, we will update this policy and our Google Play Data safety disclosures.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, sync, and restore your budget data across your signed-in devices
  • Authenticate you and secure your account
  • Generate recurring transactions and personal statistics you request in the App
  • Compute partner/household aggregate stats when accounts are linked
  • Respond to support or privacy requests
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App

3. How we share information

We share information only as needed to operate Fudget:

  • Service providers / processors. We use infrastructure providers to run the App, including Google (sign-in) and Supabase (authentication, database, and related backend services). These parties process data on our behalf under their terms and security controls.
  • Linked partner (optional). If you link with another user, that person can see combined aggregate category totals and similar summary stats. They do not receive the other person’s individual transaction rows or notes.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data storage and security

App data is stored in our cloud backend so it can sync across devices. We use HTTPS encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we take reasonable measures appropriate for a consumer budgeting app, including access controls (such as database row-level security) designed so users only access their own data except for the aggregate partner features described above.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the App. To request deletion of your account and associated app data, email kribkool@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will delete or de-identify data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

6. Children’s privacy

Fudget is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

7. International users

Fudget may be hosted or processed in countries different from where you live (including through our service providers). If you use the App, you understand your information may be processed in those locations under this policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
